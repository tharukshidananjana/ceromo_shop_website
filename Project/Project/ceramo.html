About Us

Ceramo is a gathering of multi skilled, efficient, craftspeople capable of working with various ceramic bodies and techniques to bring your ideas to life. We work with designers, manufacturers, innovators and artists from concept realization and all the way to retail shelf.

Our Story

Founded in [2003], our journey began with a passion for pottery and an appreciation for fine craftsmanship. Each piece is thoughtfully designed and carefully made by skilled artisans, ensuring unique and high-quality creations.
